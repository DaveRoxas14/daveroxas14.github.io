<span class="section-label">My Contribution</span>

Led the migration off ACS to a Knit/Component + FastCast firearm framework; built the melee weapon system, RPG launcher, roguelite Lightning/Lifesteal power-ups, the server-side ZombieManager, full mobile control support, and player-count stat scaling.

### Feature Implementation

- **New weapon framework migration** — drove the removal of the third-party ACS gun system and re-built guns on a `FirearmComponent` (Knit Component) + FastCastRedux stack; re-added zombie/wave managers and all systems on the new base.
- **Firearm content & mechanics** — added a large weapon roster (Glock19, Desert Eagle, Zastava M92, AKM/AKM-S, AKS-74U/74Un/47U-X, M249 PARA, Vector .45, Thompson, FAMAS, Lewis, SVD, RSASS, Remington 700/870, SPAS-12, Sawed-Off, M134 Minigun, P90, etc.); hipfire + separate hipfire spread, switch-weapons-while-reloading, friendly-fire toggle, KitList distribution.
- **Sniper scope system** — overhaul with 2-stage zoom, zoom-level hotkey, reticle preloading (fixed first-aim asset-streaming delay).
- **RPG / launcher system** — `Class="Launcher"` branch: AoE splash with distance falloff, configurable damage/radius/speed/mag/reload, explosion SFX, dedup by cast id.
- **Melee weapon system** — dedicated `MeleeComponent` (tag `"Melee"`), server-authoritative hit query (reach, arc, line-of-sight, point-blank handling, cooldown), combos up to 10, finisher multiplier, Machete + Axe, camera shake, hit VFX, audio; melee routed through `ToolService:Damage` so it inherits all roguelite perks. Later **hit-query hardening** (fixed backpack-swing exploit, broad-phase nearest-first target selection).
- **Roguelite Tier-3 power-ups** — **Lightning Strike** (chance-scaled AoE + stun, brand-new stun mechanic), **Lifesteal** (dual-dial chance + heal scaling, added `GetStatLevel`). Both wired in `ToolService:Damage`.
- **ZombieManager** — server-side zombie lifecycle (spawn-in-radius, track, death/despawn), per-type attribute-backed configs (Normal/Fast/Exploding/Boss with Health/Speed/Damage/Coins/Exp/CrateDrop), model variants; pathfinding improvements (waypoint staleness fix, pause-before-next-point, boss waypoint reachability).
- **Mobile support** — root-cause fix for missing movement stick (forked PlayerModule `SelectTouchModule` fallback + TouchJump/CameraController crash fixes), custom on-screen controls (Shoot/Aim/Reload/Sprint/Swing), drag-to-look-while-shooting (COD-mobile style), hold-to-autofire for semis, ADS toggle-on-mobile/hold-on-PC, center-crosshair fire fix, auto-run QoL.
- **Player-count base-stat scaling** — boosts base damage/HP/structure-HP for small groups with steep falloff, per-wave evaluation, fully configurable.
- **Custom health bar UI** + disabled default Roblox health bar; buff icons for HP-affecting powerups.
- **Loading screen + Game Over screen** — fake loading screen (once on join), game-over summary (cash/kills/wave) with kill-crediting and per-game cash accumulator wiring.
- **Disabled downed state** — replaced inherited ACS downed/revive with standard death+10s respawn, toggleable via attribute.
- **Ammo / ammo-crate system** — ammo-drop crates, refresh-ammo fixes, drop-duration/chance tuning.
- **Per-weapon ADS sensitivity** — all guns read `AimSettings.MouseSensitiveP` (was dead config).

### Notable fixes & involvements
- R6→R15 conversion estimate doc (decision: stay R6), gun-equip-after-spawn fixes, luck/muzzle fixes, in-game upgrade roll optimization, name-tag level/wave display, rope-teleport script, season challenge fixes.

<span class="section-label">About the Game</span>

Fight through endless waves of zombies, earn cash, unlock powerful weapons, and upgrade your survivor as the undead become stronger with every wave.

**UPDATE:** Welcome mobile players!

![Image](/games/2026/06/15/TFS2.webp)

### Features

- Unlock and collect a growing arsenal of weapons
- Fight with firearms and powerful melee weapons
- Roll rare weapons from the Black Market
- Upgrade your survivor with permanent perks
- Level up and become stronger every match
- Team up with other players to defend the base
- Face special infected and powerful bosses
- Survive endless waves and set new records

### How to Play

- Protect the Entrance at all costs
- Eliminate zombies before they reach the base
- Earn cash from kills and wave clears
- Purchase new weapons and upgrades
- Choose powerful upgrades between waves
- Work together to survive as long as possible

Every wave gets harder. Bosses appear every 5 waves. How long can your team survive?
